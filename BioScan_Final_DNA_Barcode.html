How BioScan Works

From biological sample to a visual DNA barcode used for identification.

01

Collect a sample

Obtain biological material from the specimen, such as tissue, hair, or another suitable source.

02

Extract DNA

DNA is isolated from the sample so a target barcode region can be examined.

03

Sequence the barcode

The selected DNA barcode region is sequenced to produce a string of A, T, G, and C bases.

04

Analyze the sequence

The sequence is checked for length, base composition, and other identifying patterns.

05

Compare with references

The barcode can be compared with validated reference sequences to support species identification.

06

Visualize the result

BioScan turns the sequence into an interactive barcode and 3D holographic specimen view.
